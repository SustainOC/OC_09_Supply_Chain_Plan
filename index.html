Why the plan is built on carbon

The compliance driver here is narrow and regional, and the STARS points sit almost entirely in operations rather than in the inventory. Three reasons survive that.

Every tonne, and who has to move it

Transportation built a mode shift matrix so the plan could be prioritized on carbon effect rather than on headline mode share. This is the same instrument for waste streams, and it produces a genuinely awkward result that the plan states rather than hides.

The levers, ranked two ways

Feasibility rank is the order the College's own audit and current state record point to. Carbon rank is the order the emission factors point to, measured against the College's own reported inventory rather than against global emissions.

How every activity is tagged

Each of the sixty three activities in this plan carries one scope tag and one action tag, with no exceptions. Reading the distribution down a year is a quick check on whether that year is balanced: a year with no Measure activities cannot report, and a year with no Abate activities is not reducing anything.

The STARS position, verified against the 3.0.1 manual

The plan level mapping of Supply Chain to STARS was written before anyone had read the 3.0.1 manual properly. It has now been checked credit by credit, and the range it named turns out to be right while the emphasis was wrong.

Thirty points sit in this impact area, the largest block anywhere in the plan. They are not evenly reachable. The credit everybody wants to talk about, the diversion rate in OP 12, is worth 5 points and Okanagan College's data cannot support it yet. The credit that attracts least attention, Materials Management in OP 11, is worth 4 points and is almost entirely a matter of writing down programs OC has run for years. A currently valid Gold peer scored 4.00 of 4 on the second while marking the first as Not Pursuing, so this is not a theoretical ranking.

The target, stated twice

One outcome in tonnes and one lever in this impact area's own unit, as every module in this plan carries. The outcome percentage is blank, and that is deliberate rather than unfinished.
